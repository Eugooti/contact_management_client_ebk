Introduction

Welcome to the Contact Management System (CMS) - your centralized platform for efficiently managing organizational contacts. This user-friendly application allows you to store, organize, search, and share contact information for various organizations and individuals.

Key Features

  • Comprehensive Contact Management: Add, edit, delete, and share contacts with ease
  • Advanced Search and Filtering: Quickly find contacts using search and category filters
  • Multiple View Options: Switch between table and card views based on your preference
  • Contact Sharing: Share contact information via email with colleagues
  • Responsive Design: Access the system from any device with a consistent experience

Getting Started

Logging In

  1. Open your web browser and navigate to the CMS application URL
  2. Enter your username and password in the login form
  3. Click the "Login" button to access the system

If you don't have login credentials, please contact your system administrator.

Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you'll be directed to the main dashboard. The dashboard provides an overview of your contacts and quick access to key features.

Main Components

  • Header: Contains the application logo, navigation menu, and user profile
  • Search Bar: Allows you to search for contacts by name, type, or other attributes
  • View Toggle: Switch between table and card views
  • Filter Tabs: Filter contacts by type (Ministries, Counties, etc.)
  • Contact List: Displays your contacts in the selected view mode

Managing Contacts

The Contact Management System organizes contacts by organization type, making it easy to find and manage specific contacts.

Contact Types

  • Presidency: Presidential and executive office contacts
  • Counties: County government contacts
  • Ministries: Government ministry contacts
  • State Departments: State department contacts
  • Parastatals: Parastatal organization contacts
  • Commissions: Commission contacts
  • Boards: Board contacts
  • Learning Institutions: Educational institution contacts
  • Private Institutions: Private sector organization contacts

Searching and Filtering

Using the Search Bar

  1. Click on the search bar at the top of the contacts page
  2. Type your search query (name, organization, etc.)
  3. Results will update automatically as you type

Using Filters

  1. Click on the filter tabs below the search bar
  2. Select a category to filter contacts (All Contacts, Presidency, Counties, etc.)
  3. The contact list will update to show only contacts of the selected type

Changing View Mode

  1. Click the list icon to view contacts in table format
  2. Click the grid icon to view contacts in card format

Contact Details

Viewing Contact Details

  1. Click on any contact in the list or card view
  2. A drawer will open on the right side showing detailed information about the contact
  3. Contact details include:
    • Organization information
    • Contact persons
    • Address information
    • Communication details

Contact Person Information

Each organization can have multiple contact persons. For each contact person, you can view:

  • Name and salutation
  • Professional title
  • Office/department
  • Email address
  • Phone number

Adding New Contacts

Adding a New Organization

  1. Click the "Add New Contact" button in the header section
  2. Select the organization type (Public or Private)
  3. Fill in the required information in the form:
    • Organization name
    • Organization type
    • Sector/industry
    • Address information
  4. Click "Next" to proceed to adding contact persons
  5. Add contact person details:
    • Name
    • Title/position
    • Contact information
  6. Review the information and click "Submit" to create the contact

Sharing Contacts

Sharing via Email

  1. Open the contact you wish to share
  2. Click on the contact person you want to share
  3. Click the share icon
  4. Enter the recipient's email address
  5. Click "Send" to share the contact information

The recipient will receive an email with the contact details in a formatted card.

Troubleshooting

Common Issues and Solutions

Issue: Unable to log in

Solution:

  • Verify your username and password
  • Check if Caps Lock is enabled
  • Contact your system administrator if the problem persists

Issue: Contact not appearing in search results

Solution:

  • Try using broader search terms
  • Check if you have the correct filter selected
  • Verify that the contact exists in the system

Issue: Unable to add a new contact

Solution:

  • Ensure all required fields are filled
  • Check your internet connection
  • Verify you have the necessary permissions

Getting Help

If you encounter any issues not covered in this manual, please contact your system administrator or the IT support team.
